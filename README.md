## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents the controlled human character from taking damage during exploration and combat.
- **Infinite Health** — Keeps the current human health pool at maximum.
- **Infinite Serum** — Maintains the serum resource used to Link with dormant Kaiju and access Beast gameplay.
- **No Link Consumption** — Reduces the resource pressure associated with maintaining a Beast Link.
- **Freeze Mission Timer** — Pauses active countdown timers in supported missions and scenarios.

### 📌 Vehicles

- **Unlimited Vehicle Health** — Protects controlled cars, helicopters, tanks, and aircraft from damage.
- **Unlimited Vehicle Fuel** — Prevents fuel depletion while operating available vehicles.
- **Vehicle Speed Boost** — Increases the movement speed of supported vehicles during exploration and combat.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Beast Invulnerability** — Prevents the active Beast from losing health during large-scale encounters.
- **Unlimited Beast Power** — Keeps the active Beast's power resources available for repeated use of its abilities.
- **One-Hit Human Enemies** — Allows human opponents in supported PvE encounters to be defeated with a single successful hit.
- **Maximum Damage** — Increases outgoing damage for supported human, vehicle, and Beast attacks.
- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents ammunition counts from decreasing for supported human and vehicle weapons.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Fast Movement** — Adjusts human movement speed for faster traversal across damaged city environments.
- **Beast Movement Boost** — Improves traversal speed while controlling a Beast across urban battlefields.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

BeastLink supports three distinct movement roles: human characters on foot, vehicle operators, and Beast Kaiju. Use Fast Movement for quicker human traversal, Vehicle Speed Boost when crossing large maps in cars or aircraft, and Beast Movement Boost when repositioning a Kaiju during city battles. Toggle movement features before entering a combat area to avoid abrupt changes during scripted transitions.

### Tools & Inventory

Serum is the key resource associated with Beast Linking. Use Infinite Serum when testing dormant Kaiju access or repeatedly entering Beast-focused gameplay. The trainer does not create fictional weapons or equipment, so ammunition and other counters are changed only when the current game build exposes a supported value.

### World & Environment

BeastLink's urban environments are built around networked, physics-based destruction. Buildings, streets, vehicles, and scenery can be damaged or destroyed, allowing players to reshape routes and use the environment during battles. Use the trainer's survivability and vehicle options when exploring heavily damaged areas or testing how different roles interact with destructible structures.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat can be experienced as a human, through vehicles, or as a linked Beast. Humans provide direct ground combat, vehicles offer mobility and fire support, and Beasts deliver powerful close-range and ability-based attacks. Use Unlimited Ammunition for supported weapons, Maximum Damage for testing attack strength, and Beast Invulnerability when evaluating Beast abilities in PvE or private sessions.
